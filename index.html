Fairness audit

Checks whether risk scores and pay gaps cluster by gender or age. The demo data includes a small pay gap on purpose, so the audit has something real to catch.

All employees, scores and salaries on this page are synthetically generated for demonstration purposes and do not represent a real company or real people. The scoring model is a transparent, weighted, rule-based model built on commonly cited attrition drivers (compensation, engagement, work-life balance, manager relationship, promotion velocity, recognition, workload, team-level attrition and onboarding stage). It is not trained on real data, so the scores illustrate explainable people-analytics design and are not predictions. Replacement cost is assumed at 1.4x salary, and intervention costs are rough estimates.
